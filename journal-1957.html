Abstract

Preliminary spin tests on an 11.3-inch copper cylinder (internal chromium-derived lining, experimental metastable alloy) have produced repeatable spacetime density modulation at harmonic multiples of 44 Hz.

At the 88 Hz threshold, a self-contained plasma toroid forms, exhibiting gravitational lensing and localized causality displacement. Backward traversal results in macro-scale expansion of the test subject relative to the target era. Forward traversal induces micro-scale compression.

Forward spin beyond the 88 Hz base risks irreversible compression. Subjects may become undetectable to the contemporary frame. Retrieval becomes mathematically improbable.
